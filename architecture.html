System Architecture

Skill Gap and Workforce Evolution Predictor, HCLTech hackathon Project 4

How the three paths work

  • Live requestA page calls api.js, a router calls its service, and the service reads MongoDB. The API serves stored forecasts and never trains during a request.
  • Offline jobThe seed script loads dummy data. The training script reads market signals, fits the local model, then stores forecasts and accuracy results.
  • Human reviewRecommendations enter the review queue and HR decides from the HR Review page. Decisions are saved with an audit entry, and rejected items are hidden from employees.
